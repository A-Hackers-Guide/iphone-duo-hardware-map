# Fact check

**Summary:** 40 claims re-derived from the primary evidence (device tree, kext plists, firmware payloads extracted fresh from the IPSW, the erase ramdisk, the dyld shared cache). **27 CONFIRMED, 8 WRONG, 4 OVERSTATED, 1 UNVERIFIABLE.** The wrong items are mostly counts and one headline claim (the three DCPs). Checked 2026-10-30.

Method notes:
- `duomap` means `python3 tools/duomap.py`. "IPSW extract" means the file was unzipped from `iPhone19,4_27.2_24B91020_Restore.ipsw` into scratch, unwrapped with `ipsw img4 im4p extract`, and then hashed or grepped. All scratch copies over 50 MB were deleted afterwards.
- The FDR sealing map came from the erase ramdisk `141-28940-105.dmg`, mounted read-only (`hdiutil attach -readonly`) and parsed with `plistlib`. The ramdisk was then detached and deleted.
- `dsc str` means `ipsw dyld str <dsc> <pattern>`, and `dsc strings` means `ipsw dyld macho <dsc> <image> --strings`.

## Other checks (all CONFIRMED)

These were also checked. They are not counted in the 40 above.

- 00: the dyld cache has 4,776 images (`ipsw dyld info`), there are 322 kexts, and the IPSW is 13,445,574,515 B.
- 01: `/arm-io/aop-gpio` compatible starts with a NUL byte. `AppleEverestErrorHandler` matches `cpu-error-handler,everest`, and no node has that compatible. `device-perf-memory-class = 0xc`.
- 02: Secure M3 is a 32-bit Mach-O with cputype 12 and subtype 15 (armv7m), `RTKit_armv7m_Oz_floatabisoft-3514.40.18`.
- 04: the `_M3` sensor drivers are exactly IMX954, IMX972, IMX915 and IMX916.
- 06: iBoot carries `ApplePMUFirmware-743.40.11~956.release`. There are five `aapl,spmi` masters. `btm` has `number-of-batteries = 2` and `number-of-PMUs = 2`, and `ppm` has `number-of-battery-packs = 2`.
- 09: vphone600 has 143 nodes, 451 of the Duo's nodes are absent from it, and the shared nodes include `/arm-io/sep/iop-sep-nub/xART`.
- 11: the N1 PCI IDs are 0x1900/0x1901, 0x1902, and 0x1903/0x1181.
- 12: `AppleSPUHapticsManagerDriver` needs `aop-haptics-manager`, and the V68 tree has no such node.
- 09, understated: the claim "the device tree does not say what `apr` is" is right as far as it goes, but the kernelcache does say. `AppleProResHW` personality `apr-p` matches `apr,p0`, the 18 Pro's `/arm-io/apr` compatible. The [INF] that `apr` is ProRes hardware can therefore become **[KC]**.

## Corrections applied

All 8 WRONG and 4 OVERSTATED items above were corrected in the chapters, FINDINGS and GLOSSARY on 2026-10-30: display controllers (only the two panel DCPs have exclave mailboxes and Mogul chips), hinge-calibration provenance (locationd via a vphone-duo guest, not the shared cache), the outer cut-out position (≈ 90%), 7 mappers, 29 thermal sensors, the IOP interrupt triples, FCAM's shared rail, 318 / 37 driver counts, the SMC rebalancer wording, pitayaOS with linked RTKit (DCP included), 57 sealing-map *entries*, and chapter 08's hinge-usage row. The `apr` node is now identified as ProRes from the kernel's `AppleProResHW` personality.
