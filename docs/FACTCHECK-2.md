# Fact check, second pass

**Summary:** 40 claims re-derived from the primary evidence. None of them was checked in the first pass ([FACTCHECK.md](FACTCHECK.md)). There are 30 claims from chapters 07, 10, 11, 12 and 13 and 10 rows of [MAP.md](MAP.md). **32 CONFIRMED, 5 WRONG, 3 OVERSTATED, 0 UNVERIFIABLE.** The most important error is a headline finding in chapter 10, which says `IOHIDEventCreateHingeAngleEvent` does not exist. It does exist, and IOKit exports it. Chapter 10 used that wrong finding to "correct" chapters 05 and 08, and chapter 11 repeats it. Checked 2026-10-30.

Method notes:
- `duomap` means `python3 tools/duomap.py`. The kext checks searched every `IOKitPersonalities` entry in `data/kexts-full.json` and the class list in `data/kernel-cpp-classes.txt`.
- **IPSW stream** means one of two things. Either the file was read straight out of `iPhone19,4_27.2_24B91020_Restore.ipsw` with `unzip -p`/`unzip -l` and parsed without writing it to disk (the C2 ftab header and `BuildManifest.plist`), or it was unzipped into `scratchpad/factcheck2/` (the Rose ftab, 1 MB). Nothing over 50 MB was written.
- **dsc str** means `ipsw dyld str <dsc> <pattern…>`. **dsc syms / dsc strings** mean `ipsw dyld macho <dsc> <full image path> -n / -s`. **dsc symaddr** means `ipsw dyld symaddr`.
- **SDK** means `/Applications/Xcode.app/…/iPhoneOS.sdk` (Xcode 27.2). Its `SDKSettings.plist` reads `DisplayName "iOS 27.2"` and `CanonicalName iphoneos27.2`, and `iPhoneOS27.2.sdk` is a symlink to it.
- **MAP rows.** Every row of MAP.md was scripted against `duomap node` for each linked node. Each claimed IOClass was then checked as either a direct node binding, or a personality or C++ class that matches by provider or HID usage. Ten rows are reported below.
